# Chapter Skill: GST Input Tax Credit (Sec 16, 17, Rules 42 & 43)

## Mandatory Statutory Evaluation Sequence
Always evaluate GST ITC problems strictly in this 4-gate sequence—if an item fails an earlier gate, stop immediately:
1. **Gate 1 — Supply & Business Nexus (Sec 16(1))**: Used or intended to be used in the course or furtherance of business.
2. **Gate 2 — Four Procedural Conditions (Sec 16(2))**:
   - `(a)` Possession of valid tax invoice / debit note (Rule 36(4): zero provisional ITC allowed; must appear in GSTR-2B under `(aa)`).
   - `(b)` Receipt of goods/services (includes "Bill-to-Ship-to" deemed receipt).
   - `(c)` Tax actually paid to government by supplier.
   - `(d)` Return furnished under Sec 39.
   - **Proviso 2 (180-Day Rule)**: Recipient must pay value + tax within 📌 **180 days** from invoice date (except RCM, Schedule I supplies without consideration, and additions under Sec 15(2)(b)), else reverse ITC with interest @ 📌 **18% p.a.** under Sec 50.
3. **Gate 3 — Time Limit & Depreciation Lock (Sec 16(3)-(4))**:
   - No ITC on the tax component if income-tax depreciation is claimed on that tax component.
   - Time limit to avail ITC: 📌 **30th November** following the end of the financial year OR date of filing annual return, whichever is earlier (for debit notes, link to the FY in which the **debit note** was issued, not the original invoice!).
4. **Gate 4 — Negative List / Blocked Credits (Sec 17(5)) & Common Credit Reversal (Rules 42/43)**:
   - Motor vehicles for passenger transport (`<= 13` seats including driver) blocked unless used for further supply, passenger transport, or driving training. Goods transport vehicles (trucks) are ALWAYS eligible.
   - Works contract / construction of immovable property on own account blocked (except plant & machinery or input service for further works contract).
   - Rule 42 (Inputs/Input Services) & Rule 43 (Capital Goods, useful life taken as 📌 **60 months**): Exempt supplies include RCM outward supplies and sale of land/completed building (stamp duty value).

## Compulsory Working Note Format for CMA Exams
Present every ITC computation as a 3-column table (`Particulars | Amount (₹) | Legal Reasoning & Section`). Never write bare numbers without a one-line statutory reason in the table or working notes.
