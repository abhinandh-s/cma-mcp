# Chapter Skill: Marginal Costing, CVP & Absorption Reconciliation

## Core Decision Protocol
Anchor every answer on **Relevant Costs**:
- **Period Costs (Fixed Overheads)**: Unavoidable in the short run unless specifically stated as "avoidable/attributable fixed cost". Never allocate general fixed overheads per unit when making short-run pricing, export order, or make-or-buy decisions.
- **Limiting / Key Factor Rule**: When machine hours, labor hours, or raw material kg are constrained, rank products by **Contribution per unit of Key Factor**, NEVER by P/V Ratio or Contribution per unit.

## Mandatory Formula & Reconciliation Spine
1. **P/V Ratio**: $\frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100 = \frac{\Delta \text{Profit}}{\Delta \text{Sales}} \times 100$
2. **Break-Even Point (Units)**: $\frac{\text{Total Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per Unit}}$
3. **Indifference Point (Units)**: $\frac{\text{Difference in Fixed Costs}}{\text{Difference in Variable Cost per Unit}}$
4. **Marginal vs. Absorption Profit Reconciliation**:
   - Difference in profit arises **solely** from fixed production overhead carried inside Opening vs. Closing Finished Goods inventory:
   $$\text{Absorption Profit} - \text{Marginal Profit} = (\text{Closing Stock Units} - \text{Opening Stock Units}) \times \text{Fixed Factory OH Absorption Rate per Unit}$$
   - **Under/Over Absorption Trap**: Always adjust Absorption Costing profit for under/over-absorbed fixed factory overhead when actual production differs from normal/budgeted capacity. Selling & Administrative overheads are NEVER inventoried in either method.
