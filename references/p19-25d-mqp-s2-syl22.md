## 6 (a) - 7 marks

Explain the provisions relating to relinquishment of title to imported goods under Section 23(2) of the Customs Act, 1962. [7][span_187](start_span)[span_187](end_span)

### Answer:

Importer may relinquish his title to the goods in the following cases [Section 23(2)]:[span_188](start_span)[span_188](end_span)
- i. The goods may not be according to the specifications;[span_189](start_span)[span_189](end_span)
- ii. The goods may have been damaged or deteriorated during voyage and as such may not be useful to the importer;[span_190](start_span)[span_190](end_span)
- iii. There might have been breach of contract and, therefore, the importer may be unwilling to take delivery of the goods.[span_191](start_span)[span_191](end_span)

In all the above cases, the goods having been imported, the liability to customs duty is imposed and, therefore, the importer may relinquish his title to the goods unconditionally and abandon them. If the importer does so, he will not be required to pay the duty amount.[span_192](start_span)[span_192](end_span)

However, the owner of any such imported goods shall not be allowed to relinquish his title to such goods regarding which an offence appears to have been committed under this Act or any other law for the time being in force.[span_193](start_span)[span_193](end_span)

**Note:** It is open to the importer to exercise the option to relinquish the title on the imported goods at any time before the passing of order for clearance for home consumption or before order permitting the deposit of goods in a warehouse.[span_194](start_span)[span_194](end_span)

## 6 (b) - 7 marks

Explain the concept of a Special Economic Zone (SEZ) and discuss the applicability of taxes and duties to SEZ units. Also, state the major incentives available to SEZ units and developers. [7][span_195](start_span)[span_195](end_span)

### Answer:

Special Economic Zone (SEZ) is a specially dedicated zone built by a nation for businesses. An SEZ is developed by a nation to promote the economic growth of the country. As the companies set up in the SEZ get relaxation on the taxes by the government and other benefits such as the incentives on export duties and ease in transportation.[span_196](start_span)[span_196](end_span)

**Applicability of Taxes and Duties to SEZ units:**[span_197](start_span)[span_197](end_span)  
SEZs are treated as foreign territory for tax purposes even though they are located within a country's borders. Supplies into SEZs are exempt from paying GST because they are considered as exports. However, when an SEZ supplies goods/services to a Domestic Tariff Area (DTA), it is exempt from paying taxes, although the receiver in the DTA has to pay IGST under reverse charge mechanism (RCM).[span_198](start_span)[span_198](end_span)

**Incentives to Special Economic Zone Units:**[span_199](start_span)[span_199](end_span)  
The government offers many incentives for companies and businesses established in SEZs. some of the important ones are:[span_200](start_span)[span_200](end_span)
- Duty-free import or domestic procurement of goods for developing, operating and maintaining SEZ units.[span_201](start_span)[span_201](end_span)
- 100% Income tax exemption on export income for SEZ units under the Income Tax Act, 1961 for first 5 years, 50% for next 5 years thereafter and 50% of the ploughed back export profit for next 5 years.[span_202](start_span)[span_202](end_span)
- Units are exempted from Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT).[span_203](start_span)[span_203](end_span)
- They were exempted from GST. Supplies to SEZs are zero-rated under the IGST Act, 2017.[span_204](start_span)[span_204](end_span)
- Single window clearance for Central and State level approvals.[span_205](start_span)[span_205](end_span)
- There is no need for a license for import.[span_206](start_span)[span_206](end_span)
- In the manufacturing sector, barring a few segments, 100% Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) is allowed.[span_207](start_span)[span_207](end_span)
- Profits earned are permitted to be repatriated freely with no need for any dividend balancing.[span_208](start_span)[span_208](end_span)
- There is no need for separate documentation for customs and export-import policy.[span_209](start_span)[span_209](end_span)
- Many SEZs offer developed plots and ready-to-use space.[span_210](start_span)[span_210](end_span)
- SEZ units are eligible for MEIS, SEIS and RoDTEP duty credit scripts.[span_211](start_span)[span_211](end_span)

Apart from the firms operating in SEZs, developers of SEZs also receive many benefits and incentives from the government.[span_212](start_span)[span_212](end_span)
