# Mathematics Model Question Paper (MQP) - 2025

## Part 1: Calculus
**Q1.** Find the integral of `f(x) = x^2 * e^x` with respect to x.
**Q2.** Determine the local maxima and minima for the function `y = x^3 - 3x + 2`.

## Part 2: Linear Algebra
**Q3.** Given matrix A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]], find its eigenvalues and the corresponding eigenvectors.
**Q4.** Prove that the determinant of an orthogonal matrix is always +1 or -1.

**Note for Tutor:** Encourage the student to show all working steps before providing the final answer.
